PART I

Federal Level Governance

Federal authority is limited strictly to core sovereign functions: national defense, foreign relations, open data infrastructure, baseline civil rights, and national credential portability.

SECTION 1

National Defense & Military Restructuring

Defense & Sovereignty
Five Operational Service Branches: Special Forces, The Guard, The Pilot Corps, The Officer Corps, and the Intelligence Branch (consolidating military intelligence, DIA, NRO, NGA, and foreign intelligence from disbanded CIA and NSA).
Civilianization of Logistics & Support: Non-combat support roles (logistics, maintenance, medical support, catering, base facilities) transition to civilian contracts paid at competitive market rates.
20-Year Defense Efficiency Mandate: Reduce defense spending by 50% over 20 years while doubling combat capability through automated force multipliers, autonomous drone swarms, and electronic warfare.
Defense-Only Deployment Mandate: Military deployments are legally restricted to defending national territory, borders, and international sea lanes. Nation-building and regime-change interventions are strictly prohibited.

SECTION 4

Credential Portability, IP & Monetary Safeguards

Innovation & Markets

Intellectual Property & Licensing Reform

Statutory Royalty Model for Patents: Eliminates 20-year legal patent monopolies. Any entity can manufacture any design from day one by paying an automated statutory micro-royalty directly to the original inventor.

Copyright Term Caps: Corporate / work-for-hire copyright term capped at 10 years. Individual creators receive up to 30 years (transferring to a corporate entity drops protection to 10 years).

Monetary Freedom & Portability

No Bailouts or Subsidies: Zero government subsidies, credits, or corporate bailouts. Entities manage risk using Information Domain data and private insurance markets.

CBDC Ban & Digital Cash Privacy: Central Bank Digital Currencies are strictly prohibited. Peer-to-peer financial freedom is guaranteed via private, encrypted open-source digital settlement protocols operating as digital cash equivalents with offline support.

Support replacing 20-year patent monopolies with automated statutory royalties?
SECTION 5

Taxation & Requisition

Direct federal income, corporate, and sales taxes are abolished. States fund the federal budget via requisitions proportional to each state's Tax Share volume. Non-defense federal lands devolve directly to host states. Military bases subject to Land Auctions generate direct credits toward host states' balances.

No direct federal income/sales taxation on individuals.

SECTION 7

Scientific Discovery, Open Research & Capital Markets

Open Science

Abolition of Grant Bureaucracy & Raw Data Mandate

Research funds flow exclusively to independent laboratories that successfully replicate findings. Basic scientific discoveries register Statutory Royalty Units (SRUs) and conduct Initial Science Offerings (ISOs) on the SDE to secure up-front capital.

PART II

State Level Governance

Domestic governance, land tenure, revenue, education, zoning, social safety nets, and local environmental stewardship are fully devolved to state jurisdiction.

SECTION 8

Revenue, Land Tenure, Shares & Property Rights (20% Land Auction Rate)

20% Auction Model
State Land Ownership & Revenue The state holds underlying territorial sovereignty over all land. All state revenue originates exclusively from 10-year blind Land Auctions (calibrated to a 20% annualized unimproved land valuation share) — eliminating income, sales, or personal property taxes.
Tax Shares & Price Stabilization Tax Share price = Total state budget divided by active Tax Shares. Annual share prices cannot fluctuate more than ±10% relative to the prior year.
10-Year Blind Land Auctions Every 10 years, land parcels are open for blind Tax Share bids. Longer leases are discounted to favor short-term (10-year) bidders. Current leaseholders have the right to match the highest bid.
Zero Carve-Outs Policy Churches, non-profits, government facilities, parks, and preserves receive no automatic exemptions from Land Auctions. Society makes intentional choices about public value by outbidding alternative uses.
Support funding state budgets via 20% unimproved Land Auctions rather than income or property tax?
SECTION 9

Zoning, Housing & Infrastructure Corridors

Urban Development
100% Private Capital Improvements Physical structures, homes, and buildings remain 100% private property and can be transferred or sold freely to new land leaseholders.
Abolition of Zoning Bans No parking mandates, density caps, floor-area ratios, or aesthetic boards. Communities wishing to preserve open space must outbid alternative development in auctions.
Linear Corridor Auctions One mid-cycle auction per decade permitted strictly for certified transit, utility, high-speed rail, or linear infrastructure projects.
Support abolishing restrictive zoning bans and parking mandates?
SECTION 10

Social Safety Net: Universal Investment Account (UIA)

$17,500 / Year
$17,500 Annual Dividend Every citizen receives $17,500 annually into a UIA target-date index fund disbursed weekly. Inflation adjusted. Full liquidity allowed anytime for any legal purpose.
Garnishment Protection Up to 50% of weekly UIA disbursement may be garnished for unpaid medical bills, legal fines, or victim restitution. Private debt garnishment is legally prohibited.
Special Needs Protection Individuals with verified special medical or developmental needs obtain 3 private insurance quotes; the state directly covers the portion of premium exceeding the annual UIA disbursement.
Legacy Program Transition Earned Social Security/Medicare discounted at 7% interest minus lifetime UIA received; remaining surplus paid out in a lump sum. Legacy administrative welfare bureaucracies are phased out.
Support replacing legacy welfare bureaucracy with a $17,500 liquid Universal Investment Account?
SECTION 11

Education & State Civic Guidance

Public schools operate on a fee-for-service model without direct operational state funding, leasing land in auctions. Families utilize UIA funds with full autonomy. Devolved state counselors provide 4 hours/year of mandatory non-biased statistical guidance on career completion rates and safety records.

SECTION 12

Externalities & Insurance-as-Regulation

Administrative regulatory codes are abolished. All entities must hold certified 3rd-party liability insurance calibrated to max potential harm with zero government backstops. Uninsured operations carry strict criminal liability and personal incarceration. CO2, noise, light, and visual clutter are classified as priced externalities.
